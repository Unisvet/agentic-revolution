A Brief History of a Grand Idea

1956

The Dartmouth Conference

The term "artificial intelligence" is coined, and the field is formally born with the optimistic premise that machines can simulate any aspect of intelligence.

2017

"Attention Is All You Need"

Google researchers publish a paper introducing the Transformer architecture. Its "self-attention mechanism" revolutionizes how models understand context, paving the way for modern LLMs.

Nov 2022

ChatGPT Ignites the AI Boom

OpenAI's ChatGPT launches, demonstrating the power of LLMs to a global audience and triggering a "code red" moment at Google, accelerating its public AI initiatives.

Dec 2023

Gemini is Announced

Google unveils Gemini, a next-generation model built from the ground up to be natively multimodal, unifying the legendary Brain and DeepMind teams.
